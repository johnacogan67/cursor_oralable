## Slide 2 — Problem (research + product)

Sleep bruxism and overnight jaw load matter in clinic. Gaps remain:

- **Rings / watches** — wrong anatomical site  
- **Clinic sEMG** — gold-standard adjacent, episodic, not overnight consumer  
- **Gap** — continuous at-home **optical + motion** record at the **temple / temporalis**

Oralable tests **extraoral temporalis OMG** (IR-DC + ACC) — **outside the mouth** — temple vitals and **bruxing / jaw-load**. That is not **intraoral** appliance sensing (incl. Dianyx t.e.s.a.). Phase 1 is **you at McGill** on methods and IEEE; Dianyx is a later talk **where complementary**.
