## 2. What the paper claims (and does not)

The paper is theory. It organizes known PSG/EMG/OSA facts; it does not measure a device or test a treatment.

| Construct | Meaning | Status in the paper |
|-----------|---------|---------------------|
| **FEP / active inference** | RMMA as a motor policy when arousal, stress, or airway cues violate expected sleep | Hypothesis-generating; FEP scope still debated |
| **Homeostatic latency** | Time from perturbation (micro-arousal, airway event, stress) back to quasi-stable EMG/autonomic baseline | Named; **not** operationalized or validated |
| **Endotypes** | Autonomic/arousal · stress/allostatic · respiratory/airway-protective · dopaminergic/basal-ganglia | Working descriptors, not diagnostic categories |
| **Behavior vs condition** | Disorder = biological cost (pain, wear), not episode count alone | Aligns with Lobbezoo consensus |
| **Hypothesis 3.5.4** | Effective MAD/CPAP should cut RMMA timed to apnea/hypopnea; leave stress- or dopamine-linked episodes relatively unchanged | Falsifiable; **untested here** |
| **Knowledge graph** | Future ontology for multimodal SB data | Explicitly not built |

Occlusal interferences are modifiers, not causes. Gold standard in *this* paper is **PSG-AV + masticatory EMG** (masseter ≥10% MVC language), not Oralable SpO₂.
