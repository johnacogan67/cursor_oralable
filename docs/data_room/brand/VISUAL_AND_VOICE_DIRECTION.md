## Look brief (paste into a chat)

**Pages:** Light only. White ground, black type, black line. Grey for quiet structure. Colour only for status (charge, worn, error). No dark mode. No purple gradients. No glow.

**Words (Orwell, 1946):** Short words. Cut what you can spare. Active voice. Concrete facts. No dying metaphors. No marketing fog (*leverage, robust, seamless, unlock, journey*). Say what is true. Keep tables, versions, FW/app IDs, and figure IDs as they are.

Break a rule only to stay clear or stay true — never to sound clever.

**Pareto:** Put most of the work on the few things that carry the claim. One job per frame. One headline. One picture that proves the point. Cut the rest.

**Pictures:** Borrow Matisse *craft*, not Matisse *subjects*. Sparse continuous black contour. White space. No cut-outs, goldfish, Great Wave, Rembrandt costume, Cubist caricature.

**Hybrid rule:**
- Reinterpret the scene as a Matisse drawing (person, room, gesture, clutter).
- Keep the **key thing you are talking about** as a real photo (or a constructed photo): device, pad, optical window, temple stack, kit piece.
- Full photo only for QC, ship evidence, or when a line drawing would hide the hardware.
- Line-only for cue-card icons and methods stubs.

**Do not:** invent “already shipped” imagery; imply intraoral product; paste patent claim art; use a full-face photo without consent.

**Yes refs (John, 8 Sep 2026):** FIG-CO-052 (hybrid) · FIG-CO-043 (line weight) · FIG-CO-051 (line portrait) · FIG-CO-050 (line hardware stub).

## 2. Visual principles

1. **Light only.** White ground. Black line and black type. Accent sparingly (app tokens — §5). Do not design a dark theme.  
2. **Person = line. Device = photo** when the frame is a product story (052 / 054 / 070).  
3. **Pareto.** One job per frame. Hero: brand or hybrid wear, one line of truth, one CTA. No stat strips on the first viewport. If a picture or paragraph does not change what the reader does or believes, drop it.  
4. **Temple is the site.** Extraoral temporalis. Do not imply intraoral product.  
5. **Honest readiness.** Kits gated until charge-to-temple. Beacon box = Oralable-only. Dual A imagery is lab.  
6. **Claim-safe IP art.** Patent-pending wording only; embodiment drawings OK; no claim text.

| Put the work here | Leave plain |
|-------------------|-------------|
| The one sentence of truth | Extra adjectives, hero clutter |
| Photo of the actual device / site | Line-drawing every fold and shadow |
| One figure a clinician can recognise | A new style per slide |
| Locked table of versions | That table rewritten as essay |

## 7. How to collect reference images

Keep the set small: **8–12 files**, not 50. Label each **Yes / No / Why**.

1. Drop files in [`docs/figures/look_refs/`](../../figures/look_refs/) (session photos can stay in `docs/figures/research_kit_photo_source/`).  
2. Keep a short `README.md` in that folder (Yes / No tables).  
3. In chat: *Look brief + `docs/figures/look_refs/`.* Attach **3–5** pictures. Attaching beats a Drive dump.

| Kind | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Hybrid you already like | FIG-CO-052, 054, 055 |
| Line weight | FIG-CO-043, 051, 050 |
| Hardware photos | Clip, case, pad, optical window, temple crop |
| Layout | A slide or app screen that is calm and light |
| Anti-refs | Dark UI, stock watches, busy decks |

Face photos: consent before public or IEEE use; prefer temple crops. Do not ask a model to reproduce a copyrighted Matisse work — point at *your* hybrids and say “this line weight.”

A constructed photograph is allowed when the real seat is messy: photograph the real device, then composite it onto the line figure (052 / 054).
