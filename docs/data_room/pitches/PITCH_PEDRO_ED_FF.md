## Slide 3 — Problem

Sleep bruxism and overnight oxygen burden matter in dental sleep medicine. But:

- **Rings / watches** — wrong site for jaw load  
- **Clinic sEMG** — accurate, episodic; not multi-night home optical  
- **HSAT tools** (e.g. AcuPebble) — strong for **AHI**; not temple jaw-load OMG  
- **Gap** — continuous at-home **optical + motion** at the **temporalis**, nestable with MAD titration
